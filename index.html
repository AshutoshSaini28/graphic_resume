Bharat Buildathon — Ideathon

August 2026
Alexa Developers Community × GeeksforGeeks CU × CSE Takshashila

A campus ideathon needed a mark that could hold its own next to a national theme without leaning on cliché tricolour stripes. I drew an original gear-and-idea-bulb symbol with a tricolour gradient running through it — building and ideation as one continuous shape.

The registration QR code carries a small Ashoka Chakra motif at its centre, and the torn-paper edges tie the poster back to the "Viksit Bharat" theme without shouting it.

Mark, layout, and poster design — original.

PPL: The Hiring Arena

September 2026
ADC × GeeksforGeeks CU × CSE Takshashila — sponsored by Unstop

A campus hiring drive rebranded as a league — so the visual language had to earn that comparison. Stadium floodlights, a navy-and-gold palette, and a trophy-shield crest carry the "arena" idea through the whole poster rather than just the headline.

The harder problem was fitting three partner logos and a sponsor mark into that system without it reading as a logo wall — solved with a single dark banner that holds all of them at equal weight.

Poster design and art direction — original. Crest mark generated from a detailed creative brief.

Campus Cup Season 2

September 2026
CSE Takshashila × Free Fire Max

An esports tournament poster built around a sponsor-supplied logo lockup — the job here was composition, not mark-making: staging the battle-royale scene, keeping the prize pool and registration QR legible against a busy background, and giving the sponsor treatment room to breathe.

High-energy source material, kept readable at a glance from across a room.

Poster layout and composition — original. Logo supplied by event sponsors.

Live Risk Map

2026
Independent Figma project — landslide early-warning dashboard concept

My one step outside print: a dashboard for a North-East India landslide early-warning system, styled after logistics-tracker interfaces. It watchlists eight districts by risk score, with a floating detail card for soil saturation, slope, and recommended action.

The reference map I started from was a dense, white-background scan that fought the dark UI. Instead of embedding it as-is, I redrew it as a low-poly vector, colour-coded by risk band — a decision that came from the interface, not the source material.

About

Third-year CSE (AI & ML) student at Chandigarh University who ended up designing more posters than most design students do — because someone had to, and it turned out to be the part of club life I liked most.

I like briefs with a real constraint: a sponsor logo I can't touch, a theme I can't ignore, a QR code that has to survive being printed on cheap paper. That's usually where the interesting decisions live.
